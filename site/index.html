Problem

The market is slow where it matters.

Distressed property opportunities often appear first in fragmented county systems, probate filings, tax lien notices, and foreclosure records. Most investors do not act on those signals until they are cleaned, normalized, and resold by lead brokers weeks later.

Why the gap exists

  • Source data appears before mainstream systems digest it.
  • Human review creates latency in decision and execution.
  • Most incumbents operate on periodic refresh cycles, not continuous reaction.
  • Speed gaps compound when downstream follow-up is fragmented.

Solution

Build a system that sees it first and moves cleanly.

Build an AI-native lead engine that monitors county sources directly, extracts addresses and contact entities from unstructured records, estimates equity, and triggers outreach before the broader market sees the opportunity.

Core workflow

Monitor public records → extract entities with AI → enrich property value and debt estimate → score equity and urgency → trigger direct mail/SMS/workflow automation.

Relevant data sources

County clerk sites, probate notices, foreclosure records, assessor/property databases, valuation APIs.

Strategic wedge

Direct county-source ingestion beats broker-lagged lead marketplaces.
