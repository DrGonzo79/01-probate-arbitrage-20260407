How It Works

From County Records to Closed Deals in Hours

Our three-stage AI pipeline turns unstructured public data into scored, actionable leads — faster than any data broker.

1

Monitor & Ingest

Firecrawl and Playwright bots continuously scan county clerk websites for new probate filings, tax liens, and foreclosure notices the moment they appear online.

2

Extract & Enrich

LLMs (Claude, GPT-4) parse unstructured PDFs to extract property addresses, heir names, and filing dates. Zillow/Redfin APIs estimate equity and confirm property value exceeds debt.

3

Score & Outreach

Leads are scored by equity, probate age, vacancy signals, and tax status. High-priority leads instantly receive personalized cash-offer letters (Lob) and SMS (Twilio).
